# TRS-80_TC-8 Advertisements Folder

Advertisements related to JPC Products TC-8 Tape System for the TRS-80 Model I

## Contents:

The below advertisements are all of the different types of advertisements I could find in 80 Microcomputing magazine,
from its inception until the disappearanc of the advertisements some time in 1983.

The general format of the advertisements was full-page, black-and-white advertisements for the TC-8 cassette system
for the Model I computer, and surprisingly, not listing any of the additional products that they sold (until they switched
focus to the Color Computer version in late 1982).

This means that they did not advertise any of the following products in their 80 Microcomputing ads, but did advertise them
either included with the actual devices that they shipped, or via separate mailings to their customer base:
- Data Cassettes
- Bus Expander ribbon cables
- EP-38 EPROM board
- FASTED patches (or any other patches they may have sold)

Their advertisements went through the following formats - advertising most months, although skipping some months at the atart:
1) "Poor Man's Floppy" in April 1980, at a price of $69.95 as a kit ($99.95 fully assembled)
2) "The Time Saver" from June 1980, with a new price - $90 as a kit ($120 fully assembled)
3) "Poor Man's Floppy" - reprint of a June 1980 review - in Novemebr 1980.  This version had a banner around the edge of the page.
4) "Poor Man's Floppy" - same as above, but without the banner around the edge of the page
5) "This Remarkable Device" (2-page ad) in March 1981
6) From November 1982, the Color Computer version was advertised in full-page
7) In April 1983, they switched to 2 quarter-page ads (for teh Color Comptuer version), shortly before discontinuing advertising.
